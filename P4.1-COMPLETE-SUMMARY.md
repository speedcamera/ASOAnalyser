## What Was Accomplished

### 1. ✅ CRITICAL BID EXPERIMENT TENANT LEAK - FIXED

**Issue:** `analyzeExperiment()` and related functions queried `daily_keyword_metrics` globally without `organisation_id` filtering.

**Risk:** Two organisations with identical keyword identities would have mixed metrics.

**Fix:** Updated 6 functions to require and use `organisationId`:
- `queryKeywordDailyRows()`
- `aggregateKeywordWindow()`
- `fetchHistoricalCpaSamples()`
- `analyzeExperiment()`
- `detectBidExperiments()`
- `detectBidExperimentsForKeywordRecords()`

**Files:** `backend/bidExperiments.js`, `backend/imports.js`

**Lines Changed:** ~200

**Result:** All bid experiment queries now tenant-scoped. Cross-tenant data leakage prevented.
